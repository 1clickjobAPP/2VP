> contract follow-up and pipeline management are **out of scope** until

## 2. Counting Rules

A deliverable is paid only if **all** of the following are true:

1. **Published & live** — visible on the public account / page at time of review.
2. **Logged in the weekly tracker** — date, link, rate. No entry = not paid.
3. **Original** — not a duplicate of the same week's post on the same platform.
   Cross-posting (e.g. IG reel reused on FB reel) counts as **one** reel,
   not two, unless it has been re-edited for the platform.
4. **On-brand** — uses 2VP branding, no spelling errors, no broken links.

The Director has final say on approval. Rejected items can be re-submitted
the following week if fixed.

## 4. Payment Process

1. **Friday 17:00** — Lilia submits the completed weekly tracker.
2. **Friday evening** — Director cross-checks each entry against the live
   accounts / website. Marks Approved (Y/N).
3. **Saturday** — Director confirms total payable amount with Lilia.
4. **Payment released** within agreed window (e.g. same day or next
   working day) once total is agreed.

No payment is made for unsubmitted weeks. Disputes are raised before the
following Monday or the week is closed.

---

## 5. What This Does NOT Cover (Yet)

To keep Phase 1 focused, the following are explicitly **excluded** from
this agreement and will be discussed once content delivery is proven:

- Lead intake / CRM logging
- Estimate preparation / calculator review
- Email follow-up sequences
- Meeting booking and coordination
- Post-contract documentation control
- Invoice / payment tracking
- Partner coordination (Certatech, Snap Website, paid ads)

When Phase 2 is opened, these will be priced and tracked separately.
