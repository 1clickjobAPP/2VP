As an office tenant, MEES binds your landlord, not you: since 1 April 2023 a landlord cannot continue to let non-domestic property below EPC E without a registered exemption.

Tenants keep asking us about stricter EPC rules. Here is the position as it stands.

Law today: the EPC E minimum, under the Energy Efficiency (Private Rented Property) (England and Wales) Regulations 2015. It has applied to new lettings and renewals since April 2018 and to all lettings since April 2023.

Not law: EPC C from 2027 and EPC B by 2030. Those are government proposals. Plan for them as a risk, but do not sign a lease on the basis that they are already in force.

Before you sign, look up the EPC and any registered exemption, and read three lease clauses: alterations, energy and EPC, and reinstatement. Your fit-out's lighting, heating, cooling and controls can move the rating either way.
