A Cat A+ office is Cat A space the landlord has already fitted with meeting rooms, a tea point, furniture and data, so you can sign a lease and move in within days rather than months.

More London landlords are pre-fitting floors this way, especially smaller ones, because teams downsizing into better space want to move fast and keep cash for the business.

It can be a good deal, but it is still a lease, not a serviced office. Before you sign, check three things in writing: who owns the fit-out, who repairs and replaces it, and what you must hand back at the end. Many leases make you return the landlord's rooms and furniture in good repair and remove your own changes. Get a schedule of condition with photos on the day you move in.

Changing the layout needs the landlord's consent, usually a licence to alter. The change that costs you least is often the one the landlord makes before you sign.
