An office kitchen fit-out is decided by drainage. Put the tea point close to a soil stack, then check your toilet numbers against the HSE ratios.

On most let London floors the soil stacks run through the landlord's core. The further your sink is from one, the more pipe, fall and risk you are paying for. On a solid floor the waste may have to run through the ceiling of the office below, which needs the landlord's consent.

Washrooms: the HSE Approved Code of Practice to the Workplace Regulations 1992 sets minimum toilets and washbasins by headcount. Count your busiest day, not your average hybrid day. Approved Document M expects an accessible toilet where toilets are provided.

Before you design anything, ask your landlord:
- Where is the drainage connection on our floor?
- Does the work need a licence to alter?
- Can the kitchen stay at the end of the lease, or must it come out?

Ask any contractor to price drainage, water, appliances and reinstatement as separate lines in writing.
