Listed building renovation in London needs Listed Building Consent for any work affecting the building's character, and unauthorised work is a criminal offence.

Listing covers the whole building, inside and out, not just the features named in the list entry. Removing walls, altering a staircase or fireplace, replacing sash windows, repointing in cement or chasing new cables through old plaster can all need consent from your council's conservation officer.

The rules are strict. Under the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, working without consent is an offence for whoever does or causes it, not knowing is no defence, and the council can make a later owner reverse the work.
