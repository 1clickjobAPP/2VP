How long does a London renovation take? On 2VP's published figures, a whole-house renovation runs 14 to 22 weeks on site, a loft conversion 10 to 14 weeks and a single-storey rear extension 14 to 20 weeks.

Those are site weeks. Before day one come design, any planning application (8-week target) or prior approval (up to 42 days), party wall notices (one to two months), and lead times: windows take 4 to 8 weeks to make, bespoke joinery 8 to 12.

Why projects run late: decisions made late, long-lead items ordered late, what an old London house hides until it is opened up, changes mid-build, and payments that run ahead of the work.

How to stay on schedule: finish the design before you sign, agree a decisions schedule with dates, order long-lead items at contract, and price every change in writing before it is built.

Combined jobs can share one programme: Amott Road in Peckham, a dormer loft plus rear and side infill extension, was 14 weeks on site.
