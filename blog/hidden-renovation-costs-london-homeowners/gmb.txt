Hidden renovation costs are the items outside the builder's price: fees, surveys, permits, VAT, contingency and living costs. Most are knowable before you sign. They just get paid to someone else.

The list London homeowners most often miss: party wall surveyors (you normally pay your neighbour's too), building control and planning fees, the structural engineer, an asbestos survey on homes built or refurbished before 2000, a drainage CCTV survey, utility upgrades, skip and scaffold licences, parking suspensions, temporary accommodation and storage, and decorating the rooms next door.

Then VAT at the standard 20% if your quote is shown NET, and contingency for what is behind the walls. The 2VP estimator carries 10% by default; for pre-1940 houses, 2VP's research puts it at 15-20%.

Ask for each one in writing before you sign.
