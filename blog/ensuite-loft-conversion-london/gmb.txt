An ensuite loft conversion works when four things check out before the layout is drawn: the drainage route, the shower headroom, the extract and the water pressure.

1. Drainage: the waste must fall to the soil stack within the branch-pipe lengths Approved Document H allows. Put the bathroom near the stack.
2. Headroom: plan the shower where you have full standing height, usually under the dormer, and put the WC and basin under the slope.
3. Extract: Approved Document F Volume 1 sets a bathroom's intermittent extract at 15 litres per second, ducted to outside, insulated through cold voids.
4. Water: test pressure and flow at the top of the house. A gravity system often needs the tank moved and a pump.

Ask your builder to show each check in writing before you choose tiles.
