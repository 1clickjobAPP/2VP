Office fit-out capital allowances give fast relief on plant like lighting, heating and cooling, and slower relief on walls and floors, so how your fit-out is split matters.

A general guide, not tax advice:
- Plant and machinery (lighting, power, heating, cooling, data cabling, sanitaryware) can qualify for the Annual Investment Allowance, full expensing for companies, or writing-down allowances.
- Many building services are integral features, in the special rate pool.
- Fixed partitions, ceilings and floors usually fall under the Structures and Buildings Allowance, spread over decades.
- VAT on a commercial fit-out is charged at the standard rate and is recoverable if you are VAT-registered and make taxable supplies.

Take advice from your accountant before you sign the contract, not after. What a contractor can give you is clean evidence: 2VP prices every fit-out as an Open-Book Itemised Quote, NET with VAT shown in addition.
