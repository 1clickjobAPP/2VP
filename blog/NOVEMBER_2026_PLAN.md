**Lessons for November:**

**What's changing in 2026 (web check, use carefully):**
- The Future Homes Standard applies to **new** homes from March 2027, not to extensions or refurbishments ([Homebuilding](https://www.homebuilding.co.uk/news/planning-reforms), [HomeOwners Alliance](https://hoa.org.uk/advice/guides-for-homeowners/i-am-improving/permitted-development-guide/)).
- Bigger permitted-development allowances were consulted on but are **not law**. Never write them as current.
- Office side: "flight to quality", downsizing into smaller, better space, and more Cat A+ fitted space ([Glenigan / Overbury](https://www.glenigan.com/boom-in-fit-out-work-set-to-keep-contractors-busy-indoors/), [GXI](https://www.gxigroup.com/blog/office-fit-out-costs-in-london-a-2026-budgeting-guide)).
