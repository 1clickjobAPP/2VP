Does a loft conversion add value in London? Usually, yes, if it adds a real double bedroom with a proper staircase and building control sign-off.

Nationwide's October 2025 analysis estimates that a loft or extension adding a large double bedroom and a bathroom can add up to 24% to a typical three-bed, one-bath house. That is a national estimate, not a valuation of your home.

On a London street the real limit is the ceiling price: what the top similar houses nearby already sell for. Before you spend, ask two or three local agents for a written value now and after the works, check recent sold prices, and get an itemised build estimate.

What drives the uplift: bedroom count, room size and headroom, a full staircase, an en-suite, daylight, and the completion certificate.
