How much office space do you need? Size it on your busiest normal day's attendance, not your headcount, then add meeting rooms, phone booths and support space before you look at any floor.

Measure attendance for four to six weeks and take the peak, not the average. If 40 people use the office and 30 are in on the busiest day, start from 30 desks and add for visitors and growth. Then count meetings from your calendars: most hybrid teams need fewer big rooms and more small rooms and booths.

Know the legal floor: the Workplace Regulations' Approved Code of Practice sets 11 cubic metres per person in a workroom. It is a minimum, not a design target. Check the measurement basis too: London office rents are usually quoted on net internal area, and 1 m² is 10.764 sq ft.

Before you sign heads of terms, ask for a test-fit on each shortlisted floor. Columns, cores and window lines decide what really fits. 2VP prices every zone on its own line in an Open-Book Itemised Quote, so you can see what one more meeting room costs.
