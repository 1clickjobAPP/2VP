Every London loft conversion needs building regulations approval, even when it needs no planning permission. Planning decides whether you may build it; building regulations decide whether it is safe to live in.

What building control checks: a new floor and steel beams designed by a structural engineer; a protected escape route down the stair with fire doors and interlinked smoke alarms, because the loft usually adds a storey more than 4.5m above the ground; stair pitch and headroom under Approved Document K; insulation under Approved Document L; extract fans and trickle vents; and certified electrics.

An open-plan ground floor needs a designed fire solution agreed before work starts. And at the end you should hold a completion or final certificate. Without it, selling the house gets harder.
