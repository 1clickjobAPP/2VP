Office refurbishment over Christmas only works if you book it in November. If your office closes after 23 December and reopens on 4 January, it is empty for 11 days, but only 4 of them are ordinary weekdays.

Everything that takes weeks has to happen first: the landlord's licence to alter, a building control application where the scope needs one, long-lead orders such as partitions and light fittings, an asbestos survey in older buildings, and written agreement on holiday access with building management. Suppliers close over Christmas too, so every item must be on site before the shutdown.

What fits a short window: redecoration, new carpet tiles, LED lighting on existing circuits, small partition changes and acoustic pods. What usually does not: new air conditioning, moved washrooms or a full strip-out. Those are better phased.
