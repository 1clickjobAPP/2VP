Office data cabling for a fit-out means Cat6A to desks and access points, a cooled comms room and landlord-approved riser routes, all agreed before the ceilings close.

Once the ceiling grid and raised floor are closed, every cabling change becomes a variation. So plan it at design stage:
- Count outlets from the furniture layout, not the headcount: desks, screens, printers, Wi-Fi access points, door readers and cameras.
- Put the comms room near the middle of the floor, so the longest copper run stays inside the 90-metre permanent link limit.
- Get riser routes and fire-stopping approved in your licence to alter.
- Order any dedicated fibre line as soon as the lease is agreed.
- Ask for the test results for every link and the labelling schedule at handover.
