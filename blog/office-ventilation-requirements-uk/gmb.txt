Office ventilation requirements in the UK come from Approved Document F: at least 10 litres per second of outdoor air per person, plus extract from WCs, tea points and print rooms.

Planning an office fit-out? Three things catch tenants out.

1. Air conditioning is not ventilation. Most split units and cassettes recirculate room air and add no outdoor air.
2. Meeting rooms go stuffy first. Size each room on its seats: an 8-seat room needs at least 80 litres per second.
3. New partitions throw the landlord's system out of balance. Every new room needs supply, extract and a return air path that does not leak sound.

Before you sign the layout, ask your landlord in writing for the design occupancy and outdoor air rate the base build was sized for, and ask your contractor for commissioning records with measured flow rates at handover.
