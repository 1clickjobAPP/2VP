An office move in London runs on three checklists at once: the lease and landlord consents, the fit-out of the new space, and the IT, people and exit from your old office.

Start with your current lease, not the new one. The break date, notice period and break conditions set the deadline for everything else. Then survey the new space before you sign, and get the licence to alter moving as soon as terms are agreed, because works cannot start without it.

Order the internet line and long-lead items such as furniture and glazed partitions early. Plan the exit as carefully as the entry: ask for the schedule of dilapidations early and agree what must be reinstated.

Before day one: a fire risk assessment for the new space, a tested network, and a handover pack from your contractor.
