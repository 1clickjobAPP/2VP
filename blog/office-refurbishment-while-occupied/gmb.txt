You can refurbish an office while your team keeps working in it, if the floor is split into phases, noisy work moves out of hours, and every escape route stays open.

Start from the business: which teams can move, when, and where to. Then zone the floor, do the shared works such as power and data first, and work zone by zone behind sealed, full-height screens.

Drilling, cutting and floor removal belong in evenings and weekends, within your landlord's building rules and the borough's. Control dust at the tool, cover return-air grilles, and take waste off site every day.

Fire safety does not pause: the fire risk assessment is reviewed for each phase, alarm isolations are reinstated before staff return, and hot works need a permit and a fire watch. In an older building, check the asbestos register before anything is opened up.
