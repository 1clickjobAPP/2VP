Accessible office design means building to Approved Document M Volume 2 when your fit-out is building work, and making reasonable adjustments under the Equality Act 2010.

Two duties, working differently. Part M is checked by building control when work is done: a fit-out must not leave access worse than before, and new doors, WCs and reception desks should meet the guidance. The Equality Act applies every day you occupy the office.

The figures a fit-out most often turns on: effective clear widths for doors, door opening force, corridor widths, at least one wheelchair-accessible unisex WC, visual contrast between walls, floors and doors, and a hearing enhancement system at reception.

Most of these cost least when they are on the first drawing. Added later, they mean taking out what you just built.

Physical changes in a rented office usually need landlord consent, so ask early and in writing.
