How to vet a builder in London: check the company, all five insurances, recent references, a live site and an itemised quote, all in writing, before you pay anything.

Start with Companies House: the company number, registered office, directors and filed accounts should match what you were given. Ask for certificates for public liability, employer's liability, contractors all-risk, professional indemnity and product liability. Check the Gas Safe register for gas work and a competent person scheme for electrical work.

Then speak to two recent clients, visit a live site, and get an itemised quote on your drawings with exclusions and provisional sums shown. Payments should follow completed stages, with retention held back at the end.

Red flags: a discount for cash, a big deposit before any work, a price with no drawings, and "you don't need party wall notices".
