Office lighting design for a fit-out comes down to four things: about 500 lux on every desk, no glare on screens, controls that switch off empty space, and a new installation that meets Part L.

The 500 lux figure comes from BS EN 12464-1, the standard most UK office lighting is designed to. Desk areas should also meet a glare rating (UGR) of 19 or lower, calculated for your room, not taken from a product brochure.

Before you move a single fitting, check three things:
- What your landlord's Cat A lighting covers, and whether your lease needs a licence to alter for changes.
- Whether the lighting must be put back at the end of the lease.
- How the emergency lighting changes once new rooms and corridors are added.

Presence or absence detection and daylight dimming are worth paying for on a hybrid floor that is often half empty. Ask any contractor to price fittings, controls, emergency lighting and testing as separate lines in writing.
