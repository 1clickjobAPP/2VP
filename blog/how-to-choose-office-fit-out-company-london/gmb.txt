Choose an office fit-out company in London on evidence: an itemised tender, named CDM roles, five insurance certificates and references you can visit.

Before you sign:
- Send three or four shortlisted companies the same tender pack: layout, specification, programme, contract form and your landlord's fit-out rules.
- Compare tenders line by line: preliminaries, M&E, provisional sums, exclusions.
- Under CDM 2015, get the principal designer and principal contractor appointed in writing.
- See certificates for all five insurances: public liability, employer's liability, contractors all-risk, professional indemnity and product liability.
- Meet the PM who will actually run the job, and call references from comparable offices.
