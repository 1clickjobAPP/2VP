The best time to renovate in London is a spring start on site, which means the paperwork starts now, in November.

Work back from a March or April start. A householder planning application has an 8-week statutory decision period once the council validates it. Party wall notices must be served up to 2 months before work to a party wall begins. A building regulations full plans decision can take 5 weeks. Design comes before all of them, and steel, windows and kitchens are made to order.

Internal work, such as a kitchen or bathroom in a weathertight house, runs fine through winter. Lofts and extensions that open the roof or back wall are easier in spring.

Timing changes cost through time, not the calendar: weather protection, drying and weekly site set-up. Ask for those as separate lines in writing.
